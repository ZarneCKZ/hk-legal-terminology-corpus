1. Origin

1. 緣起

I had my first legal translation course when I was in college, which was an elective, and I chose it because I found the professor who gave the lecture was professional and I wanted to have more of his lessons. That is where everything went wrong. This field is way more commanding than I thought, or than I could accept. I tried hard but nearly failed, and I filled in the wrong bubbles on my answer sheet. What an experience! When I saw the schedule of MA in CUHK, the course “Introduction to Legal Translation” caught my eye. “I am so going to make it this time!” I thought, with great determination, and selected the course.

我在大學時第一次修讀法律翻譯課程，那是一門選修科，我選它是因為覺得授課的教授很專業，想多上他的課。 一切就是從那裏開始出錯的。這個領域遠比我以為的、或我所能接受的更難駕馭。我盡了力，卻幾乎不及格， 還在答題紙上填錯了答案欄。多麼難忘的經歷！後來我看到中大碩士課程的時間表，一門「法律翻譯導論」吸引了 我的目光。我下定決心想着：「這次我一定要成功！」於是選了這門課。

2. Background

2. 背景

After the very first assignment, I instantly realized: I was not going to make it. The grammar was fine, though the majority of sentences were quite long and hard to read. What troubled me most were the words (or maybe the jargon), and some special uses of common words in a legal context are extremely tricky. Not only do you need to realize that such special uses exist, but you also need to identify whether a word in a given context is used in its legal meaning or another.

完成第一份功課後，我立刻意識到：這次我做不到。文法沒有問題，只是大部分句子都很長、很難讀。 最困擾我的是詞語（或者說是行內術語），而一些常用詞在法律語境中的特殊用法特別棘手。 你不單要意識到這些特殊用法存在，還要判斷某個詞在特定語境中用的是法律義還是另一種意思。

3. Gap

3. 缺口

However, the glossary lists terms and renderings row by row, but it does not show at a glance how many different Chinese renderings each English term has. I could not find a ready-made summary of how common one-term-many-renderings is in official Hong Kong legal terminology.

然而，詞彙表逐行列出術語和譯名，卻不能讓人一眼看出每個英文術語有多少種不同的中文譯名。 我找不到現成的摘要，說明「一詞多譯」在香港官方法律術語中有多普遍。

4. Questions

4. 研究問題

That is why I started this corpus. At the very start, I thought the entries would be easy to get and read. They were not. I found some of them have multiple meanings, which raised my interest: How often does one English legal term have more than one official Chinese rendering? Which terms branch into the most renderings, and does rendering multiplicity fall into a small number of clear tiers?

這正是我建立這個語料庫的原因。起初，我以為這些條目很容易取得和閱讀，但事實並非如此。 我發現其中一些有多種意思，這引起了我的興趣：一個英文法律術語有多少機會擁有多於一個官方中文譯名？ 哪些術語分支出最多的譯名？譯名多樣性又是否會落入少數幾個清晰的層級？

5. Method

5. 方法

Having downloaded the glossary from DOJ, I used the official XML from the Department of Justice, fixed its unescaped & characters, removed exact-duplicate rows (73,443 → 72,452; 991 duplicates removed), and drew 550 entries with random seed 2026, keeping only rows with non-empty English and Chinese. Each English term was classified by the number of distinct Chinese renderings it has across the full glossary: 1 → one-to-one, 2–3 → one-to-many, 4+ → polysemous. Trailing ※比較 cross-reference notes are stripped before counting (codebook v1.1). I piloted the codebook on 30 entries, revised it, then applied it to all 550. I manually checked 50 randomly sampled entries (seed 202602) against the source and found 0 errors. In brief, the codebook was revised twice: the first draft used four translation-strategy labels, but the 30-entry pilot showed that the literal / functional-equivalent boundary was too blurred to apply consistently, so I replaced it with the reproducible count of distinct Chinese renderings used here; the final v1.1 fix strips trailing cross-reference notes before counting. The full codebook change log is on the Codebook page.

從律政司下載詞彙表後，我使用律政司的官方 XML，修正了未轉義的 & 字符，刪除了完全重複的行 （73,443 → 72,452；移除 991 條重複），並以隨機種子 2026 抽取 550 條，只保留英文和中文均非空的記錄。 每個英文術語按其於整份詞彙表中擁有的不同中文譯名數目分類：1 → 單一譯法，2–3 → 多譯，4+ → 高度多譯。 計算前會先移除結尾的「※比較」互相參照註記（編碼手冊 v1.1）。我先在 30 條上試行編碼手冊，修訂後再套用於 全部 550 條。我隨機抽樣人工核對了 50 條（種子 202602），與來源比對後發現 0 個錯誤。簡言之，編碼手冊修訂了 兩次：初稿使用四種翻譯策略標籤，但 30 條試行顯示「直譯／功能對等」的界線太模糊、難以一致地套用， 因此我改為現時使用的、可重現的「不同中文譯名數目」計數；最後的 v1.1 修訂則在計算前移除結尾的互相參照註記。 完整的編碼手冊修訂紀錄見「編碼手冊」頁。

6. Analysis

6. 分析

The 550 entries split into 367 one-to-one (66.7%), 141 one-to-many with 2–3 renderings (25.6%), and 42 polysemous with 4+ renderings (7.6%). Most Hong Kong legal terms have one stable rendering, but about one in three has two or more, and a small group is highly variable (information, for example, has 6). The network graph shows which English terms branch into the most Chinese renderings; the Sankey diagram shows how one chosen term flows into its renderings; and the word cloud shows which key terms are most multiply rendered. The codebook v1.1 fix (action and statute dropped from 4 to 3 renderings after their cross-reference notes were merged) is a concrete reminder that chart numbers must be checked against the table.

550 條條目分為 367 條單一譯法（66.7%）、141 條有 2–3 種譯名的多譯（25.6%）、42 條有 4 種或以上譯名的 高度多譯（7.6%）。大部分香港法律術語都有穩定的單一譯法，但約三分之一有兩個或以上的譯名，另有一小群變化 極大（例如 information 有 6 種）。網絡圖顯示哪些英文術語分支出最多的中文譯名；桑基圖顯示所選的 一個術語如何流向其譯名；詞雲顯示哪些關鍵詞最多譯名。編碼手冊 v1.1 的修正（action 和 statute 在合併其互相參照註記後，由 4 種譯名減至 3 種）具體提醒我們：圖表數字必須與表格核對。

7. Conclusion

7. 結論

What I achieved: a reproducible 550-entry corpus drawn from the DOJ glossary; a codebook that classifies each English legal term by its number of official Chinese renderings; and an interactive site that lets visitors search, filter, and explore the 367 / 141 / 42 split through a network graph, a Sankey diagram, and a word cloud. The main finding is that most Hong Kong legal terms are stable, but roughly one in three has two or more renderings, with a small, highly variable group such as information. In a word, AI now has developed into a field that one can never imagine without using it (paid versions). However, AI still serves as an “intelligent answer machine”, waiting for good prompts to trigger its real skills. Otherwise, it is as imbecile as a traditional calculating machine, and could occasionally fail to solve one plus one. Humans in this era serve as developers, or commanders. AI is, at least currently, not here to take over the jobs, but to assist us. So, no need to dread — take the lead.

我所做到的：一個取自律政司詞彙表、可重現的 550 條語料庫；一套按每個英文法律術語的官方中文譯名數目來分類的 編碼手冊；以及一個互動網站，讓訪客搜尋、篩選，並透過網絡圖、桑基圖和詞雲探索 367／141／42 的分佈。 主要發現是：大部分香港法律術語的譯法穩定，但約三分之一有兩個或以上的譯名，另有一小群變化極大，例如 information。總而言之，人工智能已發展成一個不使用（付費版本）就難以想像的領域。然而，AI 仍像一部 「智能答題機」，需要好的提示（prompt）才能觸發它真正的能力；否則，它就愚笨得像一部傳統計算機，甚至偶爾連 一加一也算錯。這個時代的人類擔當開發者、也就是指揮官的角色。至少在現階段，AI 不是來取代工作，而是來協助 我們。所以，無須害怕——主動帶領吧。

8. Reflection

8. 反思

Additionally, due to my limited legal knowledge, deciding the category troubled me a lot and was done completely by AI; though I found it unsatisfactory, I could not raise a better plan. But I did learn how to have continuous conversations with AI so that it could develop and better the project step by step, though sometimes it would forget what we had done and what should be kept, which was a real headache for me. If given another chance, I would, first and foremost, have learnt legal English better back in college, so that I could have done better when I decided how to define the categories. Though AI did help a lot, I am still uncertain about the feasibility of such a category. To put it plainly, what I learned is how to steer an AI through an iterative task and how to verify its numbers; what stayed hardest was my own legal-terminology judgement; what I would do differently is learn legal English earlier and pilot the categories even sooner; and what AI could do was draft, classify, and visualise quickly, while what it could not do was guarantee that its classifications were conceptually right.

此外，由於我的法律知識有限，決定分類一直令我十分苦惱，最後完全交由 AI 完成；雖然我覺得結果並不理想， 卻提不出更好的方案。但我確實學會了如何與 AI 持續對話，讓它一步一步把項目發展得更好，雖然它有時會忘記 我們做過甚麼、甚麼應該保留，這真的令我頭痛。如果再有機會，我會首先在大學時把法律英語學得更好，這樣在 決定如何界定分類時就能做得更好。雖然 AI 幫了很大的忙，我對這種分類是否可行仍不完全確定。說得明白一點： 我學到的是如何引導 AI 完成一項反覆迭代的工作，以及如何核對它的數字；最困難的始終是我自己對法律術語的 判斷；我會做不同的是更早學好法律英語、更早試行分類；AI 能做的是快速起草、分類和視覺化，而它做不到的是 保證其分類在概念上是正確的。
